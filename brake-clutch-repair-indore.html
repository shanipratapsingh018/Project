Brake and clutch repair

Brakes and clutch wear faster in stop-start city traffic. We inspect, show you the worn parts, and replace them with the parts grade you choose.

Estimate my costWhatsApp usCall +91 89659 57978

Brake pads typically need checking every service. Squealing, grinding or a soft pedal mean they should be checked right away.

A slipping clutch, hard pedal or burning smell points to clutch wear. Clutch work involves removing the gearbox, so we confirm the full quote first.

Typical problems & symptoms

  • Squeal or grinding when braking
  • Brake pedal feels soft or goes low
  • Car pulls to one side under braking
  • Clutch slips, judders or the pedal is hard

What's included in front brake pad replacement

  • Replace front pads, clean & lube calipers
  • Brake pad set (standard grade)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my brake pads are worn?

Squealing, grinding, longer stopping distances or a brake warning light. We measure pad thickness during inspection and show you.

Do you replace discs too?

Disc skimming or replacement isn't in the brake estimate. If discs are worn we quote them separately.

Can you repair automatic transmissions?

Automatic and AMT transmission problems need inspection first. Select transmission repairs in the estimator to request a quote.
