FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why is the suspension range so wide?

It depends on whether a single link, bushes or full shock absorbers need replacing. We show you the worn parts before quoting.

Are new tyres included?

No. New tyres are quoted separately by size and brand.

How often should I get wheel alignment?

Every 5,000 to 10,000 km, after hitting a deep pothole, or after suspension or steering work.
