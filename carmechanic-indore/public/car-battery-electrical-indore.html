Battery and electrical services

A weak battery or an electrical fault can leave you stranded. We test the battery and charging system, trace wiring faults and fix lights, switches and power windows.

Estimate my costWhatsApp usCall +91 89659 57978

Most car batteries last 3 to 5 years. Slow cranking on winter mornings is often the first sign.

For electric cars, battery service here means the 12V auxiliary battery. High-voltage traction battery work is not included.

Typical problems & symptoms

  • Engine cranks slowly or not at all
  • Battery warning light
  • Lights flicker or are dim
  • Power windows or central locking not working

What's included in battery replacement

  • New battery with fitting and old-battery disposal
  • Battery (brand/capacity per model), fitting

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will you test the battery before replacing it?

Yes. We test battery health and the charging system first so you don't replace a good battery.

Which battery brands do you fit?

Tell us your preference. The battery brand and warranty from the battery maker are written on your bill.

Do you fix EV traction batteries?

No. We handle the 12V auxiliary battery and general electricals on EVs.
