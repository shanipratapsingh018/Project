    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the price shown by the calculator final?

    No. The calculator gives an estimated range. Your final quotation is shared after a mechanic inspects the car, and no extra work is done without your approval.

    Do you service all car brands?

    We are a multi-brand garage. Tell us your car's make, model and year on WhatsApp and we will confirm before you book.

    Is pickup and drop really free?

    Pickup and drop is free within our confirmed Indore service area, subject to booking confirmation. Enter your PIN code on the Pickup & Drop page to check your area.

    Can I choose genuine (OEM) parts?

    Yes. The estimator lets you compare economy, standard and genuine parts. The parts used are written on your itemised bill.

    What if the mechanic finds another problem?

    We call or WhatsApp you with the issue and the extra cost first. Work starts only after you approve it.

    How do I pay?

    Payment terms are shared with your final quotation. You receive an itemised bill listing labour and parts separately.
